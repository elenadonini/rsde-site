What we do

RSDE is a research unit of the Digital Society Center at Fondazione Bruno Kessler, founded on 1 July 2013. We work on pattern recognition, machine and deep learning, domain adaptation and transfer learning for multispectral, hyperspectral, SAR and radar sounder data.

Vision

Understanding other planets is a way to learn more about our own, and about its future.

Approach

Research

New methods for multitemporal, multisensor image analysis: change detection, segmentation, domain adaptation.

Develop

End-to-end processing chains, from raw satellite, airborne and radar sounder data to maps and climate variables.

Apply

Climate services, agriculture, forestry, infrastructures, civil protection and planetary science missions.

Four research directions

01 · Earth

Environmental monitoring & climate change

Estimating the variables that describe how the Earth changes, and why.

  • Land use / land cover mapping
  • Time-series change detection
  • Fires and floods
  • Cryosphere and water quality
02 · Space

Planetary exploration

Design of radar sounder systems and analysis of data from planetary missions.

  • RIME on ESA JUICE (Jupiter's icy moons)
  • SRS on ESA EnVision (Venus)
  • Automatic radargram analysis
03 · Society

Infrastructures, resources & planning

Remote sensing analysis deployed where decisions are made.

  • Infrastructure monitoring
  • Disaster assessment
  • Precision agriculture
  • Forest inventory from lidar
04 · Open

Open & crowdsourced data

With the Digital Commons Lab, we combine satellite imagery with OpenStreetMap and open geodata.

  • Semantic segmentation with noisy labels
  • Map updating
  • Disaster impact assessment
Application areas Precision agricultureForestryWater resources Infrastructure monitoringCivil protectionClimate services

Products, maps & data

Some of the datasets, maps and processing chains coming out of our research.

Dataset · ESA CCI

CCI High Resolution Land Cover products

High-resolution land cover and land cover change maps from Sentinel time series, released in Phase 1 of the ESA CCI HRLC project.
Method · Sentinel-2

Burned area detection & tracking

Automatic mapping of fire-affected areas over time, applied to the 2023 fires in Rhodes, Corfu and Palermo.

Maps · Sentinel-2 / Landsat-9

Water quality of Italian lakes

Total suspended matter and chlorophyll-a retrieved from multispectral imagery for Lake Garda and other subalpine lakes.

Method · Sentinel-2 SITS

Crop phenology for precision agriculture

Estimation of phenological parameters and large-scale field mapping from satellite image time series.

Method · LiDAR

Forest inventory from laser scanning

Tree canopy segmentation and detection of sub-dominant trees from airborne lidar point clouds.

Method · SAR

Building change detection

Collapsed buildings detected in very high resolution SAR images after the 2009 L'Aquila earthquake.
